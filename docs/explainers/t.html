Daily mood

One draw per simulated day. MtMt The day's mood. Outputs an integer in 0…N. Every behavioural channel is a deterministic function of this value and the clock.engine/mood.py :: step is beta-binomial: four additive terms form a logit argument, the logistic function maps it to a probability, and N Bernoulli trials produce the integer.

Provenance of the anchors

The five anchor values are product parameters chosen by the team, not estimates from data. The phase-contrast experiment verifies that the implementation reproduces them; it does not test them against measurements of people. The cited literature reports small effects with wide between-person variation.

Memory and drift

Two AR(1)first-order autoregressive processxt+1 = ρxt + input + noise. Each step keeps a fraction ρ of the previous value, so shocks decay geometrically with half-life ln½/lnρ. processes on the deviation terms. μt is driven by the daily score; ηt is driven by noise only.

Proposed variants

Three alternatives to the frozen model. None implemented.

variant not implemented

Lognormal renewal

The Weibull hazard with kw>1 is monotone increasing and unbounded in τ. The lognormal hazard rises to a maximum and then decreases.

h(τ)= φ(z) τσln [1−Φ(z)] , z= lnτ−μln σln

φstandard normal densityφ(z) = e−z²/2 / √(2π). The bell curve with mean 0 and variance 1. and Φstandard normal CDFΦ(z) = ∫−∞z φ(u) du. Probability that a standard normal is below z. 1 − Φ(z) is the survival function, which is the denominator of the hazard. · μln set so the median matches the current θ · σln shape. Empirical inter-event times in human messaging are reported as heavy-tailed with burstiness persisting after removing daily and weekly periodicity.

State factor Sd and the measured effect

Sd= clip[ exp(∑i=14 wi(xd,i −x0,i)) ,0.5,2]

xd = (energy, initiative, valence, reactivity) · x0 = (0.5, 0.5, 0, 0.5) · w = (1.0, 1.6, 0.7, 0.9). Under the ablation that flattens the state, xd=x0 and Sd=1 exactly.

weights as built weights scaled

Across the saturation range of μ the monthly mean of Sd varies by about 70%. The measured difference in realised event count between the state-on and state-off conditions is 1.35% over five seeds.

One candidate explanation was tested and rejected: with the tail guards removed, a rate multiplier of 1.5 still produces about 21% more events, so the guards are not absorbing the difference. The location of the loss is not established.
